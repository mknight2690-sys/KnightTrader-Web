Why I built it this way

Most trading bots I tried had two problems. You had to leave your own computer running, browser open, VPN on, all night. And when a trade went bad, they'd "average down": keep buying more of a loser and hope it comes back, with no stop. That works right up until the day it doesn't, and then it can take the whole account. I wanted the opposite, so I built it.

The network: one shared memory, secured like proof of stake

Every part of KnightTrader writes what it learns to one shared memory ledger, memory.md. Before anything is added, the rest of the system has to agree it's true.

Every component is a validator

The trading engine, each bot, the strategy builder, the AI and Medic all take part. Each earns stake from its verified track record.

Stake-weighted validation

Any validator can propose an entry: a lesson, a strategy result, a promotion or a repair. It commits only after enough stake-weighted validators check it against real data and approve.

Append-only, hash-chained

Approved entries become blocks linked by hash, so past entries can't be quietly edited.

Being wrong costs stake

A validator whose entries later prove wrong loses stake, so it carries less weight next time.

"Proof of stake" describes how entries are validated inside KnightTrader. There's no token, no staking of your money and no yield. Better-informed decisions are not guaranteed profits.

Medic: repairs that stay fixed

Medic watches the whole system around the clock. When something stalls or breaks, it doesn't just restart and hope. It finds the root cause, logs it, and applies a permanent fix so the same problem doesn't come back.

Risk rules enforced in code

These limits are checked in code before an order reaches your exchange. An order that breaks them is rejected.

  • Take-profit and stop-loss on every position.
  • Minimum reward-to-risk of 2.5: a trade that risks $1 must target at least $2.50.
  • Isolated margin with capped leverage, so one trade can't drain the rest of the account.
  • Hard ceiling on position size as a percentage of your account.
  • Pump filter that skips coins that have already gone vertical.
Trading involves risk. Crypto and leveraged trading can lose money quickly, including your whole deposit. Start on paper, and only trade live with money you can afford to lose.

Your keys, your exchange

KnightTrader never holds your funds. Live trading needs a trading API key from your own exchange account, stored encrypted on the server. Keep withdrawals off on that key, so even a leaked key can't move money out.

FAQ

Can I try it without real money?

Yes. Switch to Paper, choose a starting balance, and it trades that at live market prices. The first run starts right away.

How often does it trade?

It reviews the market every 15 minutes, on the server, so your computer or phone can be off. It may decide not to trade.

Does KnightTrader hold my money?

No. Funds stay on your exchange. For live trading you add a trading API key with withdrawals off.

Which exchanges?

BloFin and Coinbase. You must be eligible to use the exchange under its own terms.

Will it make money?

Nobody can promise that. It trades within fixed limits, and it will lose some trades. Paper results don't guarantee live results.

What does Lifetime Founder mean?

One payment of $1,497 gives your account paid access for as long as KnightTrader is offered, with no monthly bill. Only 25 seats.

Why can't it skip a stop-loss?

The risk check runs in code before any order reaches your exchange. An order without a stop, or under 2.5 to 1 reward-to-risk, is rejected.

Can I stop it?

Yes. Pause trading or delete your key at any time, and cancel a monthly or annual plan anytime through Stripe.
