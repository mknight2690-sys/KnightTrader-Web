How it works, start to finish

1. Create your account

Sign up with email or Google. Each person gets their own account, keys, memory and history.

2. Connect your exchange

The guided setup page walks you through making a BloFin or Coinbase Advanced API key with trading on and withdrawals off, then checks that the key works.

3. Hermes takes the shift

Every 5 minutes, on our server, Hermes reads the market and your account, decides, and acts, or deliberately does nothing. Your computer can be off.

4. You watch and talk to it

Follow it live on the dashboard, and ask it why it took a trade on its own chat page.

All you need is a phone

No PC, no server, nothing to install. Open the BloFin or Coinbase app, verify your ID, fund your account, and create an API key with trading on and withdrawals off. Then paste it into KnightTrader from your phone's browser. We run the servers that host Hermes, so it keeps trading around the clock even with your phone locked in your pocket. The dashboard, the live equity curve, voice alerts and the chat with your Hermes all work on a phone screen.

Risk rules the AI can't talk its way around

An AI can be persuasive. These limits aren't suggestions in a prompt. They're checked in code before any order reaches your exchange, and an order that breaks them is rejected.

  • Mandatory take-profit and stop-loss on every position.
  • Reward-to-risk of at least 2.5. A trade that risks $1 must target $2.50 or more.
  • 5x leverage, isolated margin, so one bad trade can't drain the rest of the account.
  • Percent sizing with a hard 10% ceiling. Hermes sizes each trade to your account and can never put more than 10% of it in one trade.
  • Pump filter that skips coins that have already gone vertical.

Encrypted at rest, quantum-resistant by design

Your API keys, Hermes memories and chats are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The master key is sealed to the server, not stored next to the data. Passwords are hashed with scrypt, so we never keep them in readable form.

Why AES-256: the best known quantum attack on symmetric encryption (Grover's algorithm) only halves its effective strength, which leaves about 128-bit security. That's still far beyond reach. So stored data is quantum-resistant at rest. To be precise: the connection between your browser and the site uses standard HTTPS, which isn't post-quantum yet.

And because withdrawals stay off on your key, even a leaked key can't move your money out of the exchange.

FAQ

Does KnightTrader hold my money?

No. Funds stay on your exchange account. We store a trading API key, encrypted, and the setup guide has you leave withdrawals off.

Do I need to keep my computer on?

No. Hermes runs on our server every 5 minutes.

Which exchanges?

BloFin and Coinbase Advanced. You must be eligible to use the exchange under its own terms.

Will it make money?

Nobody can promise that. It will trade with fixed limits, and it will lose some trades. Start with an amount you can afford to lose.

Can I stop it?

Yes. Pause trading or delete your key at any time, and cancel the subscription anytime through Stripe.
